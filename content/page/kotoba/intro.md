Vocabulary comes before grammar. This is the lesson from my English learning journey — memorizing words first made grammar easier to absorb.

## Why Kotoba First?

If you already know what a word means, grammar becomes about *how* to use it, not *what* it is. Learning grammar with unknown vocabulary is like reading a map in a language you don't speak.

## How This Section Is Organized

Each list contains 20 foundational words. No example sentences yet — that comes in Bunpō. The goal is simple recognition and recall.

- **List 1:** Greetings, numbers 1–10, pronouns, yes/no.
- **List 2 (planned):** Family, colors, days of the week.
- **List 3 (planned):** Food, places, common verbs.

## How to Study a List

1. Read each word aloud.
2. Cover the romaji — can you recall the meaning from the kana alone?
3. Cover the kana — can you recall the word from the meaning?
4. Write the kana from memory.

No deadline. Recognition first, recall second, writing third.
