Let's go to our first place, Hiragana, the basic of Japanese, all the Japanese word is using this Character or letter... with a lot of rounding and spinning pen move, Hiragana is still easy to get used to. there's 46 basic Hiragana letters (With total up to 71)... You think that's a lot? Nah bro

2. Katakana

Please don't confuse Katakana with Katana (Japanese Sword), BUT!! The two of them has similarity... If Hiragana is rounded and spin spin, then Katakana is all about SHARPNESS... How sharp your character, and could it slash something, kidding. Katakana is used for foreign word that forced to speak and write in Japanese.

3. Furigana

It's chibi (Child body) version of Hiragana, their function is to make Kanji readable, and of course their parents is the one and only... Kanji
