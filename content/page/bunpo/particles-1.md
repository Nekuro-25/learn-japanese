Five particles cover most beginner sentences. Learn the *role* of each one, then the sound.

## は (wa) — Topic Marker

Marks what the sentence is *about*. Pronounced "wa", not "ha".

- <span lang="ja">わたしは がくせい です。</span> — *Watashi wa gakusei desu.* — I am a student.
- <span lang="ja">これは ほん です。</span> — *Kore wa hon desu.* — This is a book.

The sentence is "as for X, it is Y". The topic is already established or being pointed to.

## が (ga) — Subject Marker

Marks *who does the action* or *what has the quality*. Often emphasizes new information.

- <span lang="ja">ねこが すき です。</span> — *Neko ga suki desu.* — Cats are liked (by me).
- <span lang="ja">だれが きましたか。</span> — *Dare ga kimashita ka.* — Who came?

Compare: <span lang="ja">わたしは ねこが すき です。</span> — "As for me, cats are liked." The は marks the topic (me), the が marks the object of liking (cats).

## を (o) — Object Marker

Marks the *direct object* of an action. Pronounced "o", same as <span lang="ja">お</span>.

- <span lang="ja">りんごを たべます。</span> — *Ringo o tabemasu.* — I eat an apple.
- <span lang="ja">ほんを よみます。</span> — *Hon o yomimasu.* — I read a book.

## に (ni) — Destination, Time, Target

Marks a *point*: where you're going, when something happens, or who receives something.

- <span lang="ja">がっこうに いきます。</span> — *Gakkou ni ikimasu.* — I go to school.
- <span lang="ja">しちじに おきます。</span> — *Shichi-ji ni okimasu.* — I wake at seven o'clock.
- <span lang="ja">ともだちに あげます。</span> — *Tomodachi ni agemasu.* — I give (it) to a friend.

## で (de) — Location, Means

Marks *where* an action happens or *what means* you use.

- <span lang="ja">がっこうで べんきょうします。</span> — *Gakkou de benkyou shimasu.* — I study at school.
- <span lang="ja">でんしゃで いきます。</span> — *Densha de ikimasu.* — I go by train.

Compare に vs で for "school":

- <span lang="ja">がっこうに いきます。</span> — I go **to** school. (destination)
- <span lang="ja">がっこうで べんきょうします。</span> — I study **at** school. (location of action)

## Quick Reference

- **は** — as for X (topic)
- **が** — X (subject / emphasized)
- **を** — X (object)
- **に** — to / at / on X (point)
- **で** — at X / by X (location of action / means)

## Practice

Read these aloud and identify the particle role in each:

- <span lang="ja">わたしは にほんごを べんきょうします。</span> — I study Japanese.
- <span lang="ja">ねこが だいどころに います。</span> — A cat is in the kitchen.
- <span lang="ja">でんしゃで とうきょうに いきます。</span> — I go to Tokyo by train.

That's it for Particles 1. Once these five feel natural, everything else builds on top.
